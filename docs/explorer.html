Explore: Household Debt & Policy Effectiveness

Two of my papers in Macroeconomic Dynamics reach a striking pair of conclusions: fiscal stimulus works better when households carry little debt, while rate cuts hit harder — but fade faster — when they carry a lot. Use the controls below to switch between the two policies and across countries, and watch how the level of household debt changes the response of output.
