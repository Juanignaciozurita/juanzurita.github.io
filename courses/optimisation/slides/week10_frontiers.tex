\begin{frame}{Stochastic Dynamic Programming}
Real economies face uncertainty: $V(k,z) = \max_c\{u(c) + \beta\,\mathbb{E}[V(k',z')|z]\}$\\\bigskip $z$ follows a Markov process (e.g.\ productivity shocks).\\\bigskip Discretise with Tauchen or Rouwenhorst methods; solve with VFI over the joint $(k,z)$ grid.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Stochastic Gradient Descent}
In machine learning: $\min_\theta\frac{1}{N}\sum_i\ell(\theta;x_i,y_i)$\\\bigskip SGD uses a random \textbf{mini-batch} instead of all $N$ samples:\\ $\theta_{t+1} = \theta_t - \alpha_t\nabla\ell(\theta_t; x_{i_t}, y_{i_t})$\\\bigskip Variants: momentum, Adam, RMSProp --- foundations of deep learning.
\end{frame}
